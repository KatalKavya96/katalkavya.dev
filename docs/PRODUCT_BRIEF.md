# Product Brief

## Product

A modern personal engineering portfolio for Kavya.

## Primary goal

Help companies, engineers, founders and collaborators understand Kavya's career direction and proof of work quickly, while allowing technically curious visitors to go much deeper.

## Core positioning

The site should communicate Kavya's actual areas of work after they are confirmed. The current design supports projects, open-source work, technical experimentation, and coding profiles without claiming experience in any specific field.

The portfolio must show breadth without making the visitor work through a cluttered catalogue.

## Visitor questions the site must answer

Within ~30 seconds:

1. Who is Kavya professionally?
2. What kinds of systems does Kavya build?
3. What are the strongest examples?
4. Is there real-world/open-source proof?
5. Where can I inspect the code or contact Kavya?

Within ~3 minutes:

1. How does Kavya think about architecture and trade-offs?
2. What did Kavya personally contribute?
3. What has shipped or been merged?
4. What is Kavya actively exploring?
5. Does Kavya's work align with the role/team I have in mind?

## Audience priority

1. Engineering hiring managers and technical interviewers
2. AI/ML / agentic AI teams
3. Open-source maintainers and collaborators
4. Startup/founder/product teams
5. Recruiters
6. Other developers and peers

## Desired impression

Calm, serious, technically curious, high-agency, product-aware and evidence-driven.

The site should not state that Kavya is a "must-have" candidate. It should present enough proof for visitors to reach their own conclusion.

## Narrative

The portfolio should progressively reveal depth:

**Identity → Work → Proof → Engineering depth → Code → Journey**

## Content pillars

### Build

Flagship projects and shipped products.

### Contribute

Open-source contributions, organizations and pull requests.

### Explore

Experiments, labs and technical investigations.

### Practice

Coding/problem-solving profiles and consistent development activity.

### Think

Architecture decisions, trade-offs, failures and lessons.

## Anti-goals

Do not become:

- a resume pasted into a website,
- a GitHub clone,
- a dashboard full of counters,
- a cyberpunk/neon template,
- a giant list of technologies,
- a certificate gallery,
- or an animation demo with weak content.
